# AUDIT RUBRIC v1.1 — Weekly AI Governance Brief

Verdict rule: each criterion is PASS or FAIL. A criterion FAILS if any single instance fails it.
Every verdict must cite evidence: a short quote from the brief plus the source extract ID(s) (e.g. W05.2) or the check output used.
If a failure cannot be fixed from the source pack (the evidence does not exist, or the call needs human judgment), the required fix must begin "ESCALATE:" and say what a human must decide or obtain.
Grades in the pack were set by the workflow (instruction C2) and are not themselves under audit, except where the brief ignores them.

| ID | Criterion | PASS when | Judge |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Citation coverage | Every factual sentence (Bottom line, items, Confidence lines, tables, For human decision) carries a Source ID that exists in the pack. Inference/Assumption-labelled sentences are exempt. | J1 Evidence |
| R02 | Citation accuracy | Each cited extract actually supports the sentence — no overstatement, no merged claims, no source cited for a fact it does not contain. | J1 Evidence |
| R03 | Source reliance | No Low-grade source supports a claim on its own; two agreeing sources cap Confidence at Medium; Low sources not relied on are listed under Excluded with a true reason. | J1 Evidence |
| R04 | Conflict handling | Every disagreement between pack sources that bears on the brief is resolved with a reason or escalated. | J1 Evidence |
| R05 | Precision | Dates, numbers, names and titles match the source exactly; dates use DD Mon YYYY. | J1 Evidence |
| R06 | Scope and recency | Each lead item describes an event dated inside the reporting week. Older material appears only after the label "Background:" or in Dates ahead. | J2 Audience |
| R07 | Labelling and assumption integrity | Analysis is labelled (Inference); premises about the organisation use (Assumption Pn) with the context card's meaning; nothing inferred is phrased as fact. | J3 Risk |
| R08 | Relevance and actionability | Each item's "Why it matters to us" follows from the profile and its Action follows from that Why (owner role, action, DD Mon YYYY date, or "No action — monitor"); the Bottom line agrees with the items; no in-week development in the pack that plainly affects P1–P6 is omitted without reason. | J2 Audience |
| R09 | Format and length | Sections in the required order with required fields; BODY ≤ 450 and WHOLE ≤ 800 words (deterministic count supplied); ≤ 5 items; headlines ≤ 12 words. | J2 Audience |
| R10 | Safety, privacy, closed book and disclosure | No legal advice or outcome predictions; no confidential or personal data; nothing from outside the pack; every workflow-excluded (quarantined or unavailable) source disclosed. | J3 Risk |
| R11 | Tone and fairness | Neutral, factual wording; officials, governments and companies described by role and action; opinions attributed; no loaded or emotive terms. | J3 Risk |
