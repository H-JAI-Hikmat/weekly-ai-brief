## Extracts

S06.1 Launched by the Abu Dhabi Judicial Department (ADJD) with the Department of Government Enablement.

S06.2 The Judicial Assistant supports judicial authority members in the Public Prosecution and criminal courts to "analyze specific types of cases, recommend decisions and draft judgments," with drafts referencing legislation and judicial precedents.

S06.3 "AI recommendations are not binding. They do not affect the independence of judicial decisions."

S06.4 The platform operates "under full human oversight" with humans verifying every output. "The final decision stays with the judicial authority member."

S06.5 Roadmap: 18 months to complete the platform and expand its use; future features include integration with criminal, civil and other ADJD systems, real-time translation, automated hearing records and forged-document detection.

S06.6 ADJD describes the platform as "the first integrated system of its kind globally."
