## What changed this week

### 1. FTC opens industry-wide probe of AI labs — US — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: On 30 Sep 2026 an FTC official told Reuters of an industry-wide probe of Anthropic, OpenAI and other labs; Reuters described it as focused on rogue AI agents [S05].
- Why it matters to us: If our US API providers are among them (Assumption P2), their agent terms may change (Inference).
- Action: Head of Information Security · confirm agent pilots are sandboxed and logged · by 19 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — one roundup relaying Reuters [S05].

### 2. Freshfields: UAE has no AI-specific federal law — UAE — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: On 28 Sep 2026 Freshfields published a table showing no AI-specific UAE federal law enacted or in the pipeline (data: 01 Jun 2026) [S01]. Background: Federal Authority for AI and Data announced 14 Jun 2026 [S10][S09].
- Why it matters to us: Exposure likely comes via existing data, sector and vendor rules (Inference); sector rules apply if Central Bank, DIFC or ADGM licensed (Assumption P6).
- Action: Head of IT Risk · confirm P4 and P6 with Legal · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — data predates the Authority [S01][S10]. We reject the conflicting "UAE AI Act 2026" claim [S13]; see Excluded.

### 3. US order renames AI "Super Intelligence"; voluntary accord signed — US — Relevance: Low
- What happened: On 29 Sep 2026 an executive order (US presidential directive) told agencies to replace "AI" with "SI" (Super Intelligence) in official documents [S02][S03]. Tech leaders signed an accord creating no legally enforceable obligations [S03].
- Why it matters to us: Without US federal contracts (Assumption P5), likely no effect (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: High — order and law firm agree [S02][S03]. S04 says the accord "requires" controls; we rely on law-firm S03 [S04].

### 4. Abu Dhabi launches Judicial AI Platform — UAE — Relevance: Low
- What happened: On 28 Sep 2026 Abu Dhabi's Judicial Department launched an AI platform; recommendations are non-binding and humans verify outputs [S06][S07].
- Why it matters to us: Public-sector deployment, not a rule binding us (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: Medium — two outlets agree; neither names an author [S06][S07].
