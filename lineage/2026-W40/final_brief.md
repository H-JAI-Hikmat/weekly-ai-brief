- Why it matters to us: Our agent pilots can call internal tools (P3); Reuters tied the probe to rogue agents [S05]. If our US providers (P2) include Anthropic or OpenAI (unconfirmed), their agent terms may change (Inference).
- Action: Head of Information Security · confirm agent pilots are sandboxed and logged · by 19 Oct 2026.

### 2. US order renames AI "Super Intelligence"; voluntary accord signed — US — Relevance: Low
- What happened: On 29 Sep 2026 an executive order (US presidential directive) told agencies to replace "AI" with "SI" (Super Intelligence) in official documents [S02][S03]. Tech leaders signed an accord creating no legally enforceable obligations [S03].
- Why it matters to us: Without US federal contracts or grants (Assumption P5), likely no effect (Inference). If our US providers (P2) include accord signatories (unconfirmed), their commitments are voluntary [S03] (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: High — order and law firm agree [S02][S03]. S04 says the accord requires controls; we rely on law-firm S03 and exclude S04 (Low) [S04].

### 3. Abu Dhabi launches Judicial AI Platform — UAE — Relevance: Low
- What happened: On 28 Sep 2026 the Abu Dhabi Judicial Department (ADJD) launched an AI platform [S06][S07], designed to run under "full human supervision and validation" [S15]. Background: the UAE had no AI-specific federal law at 01 Jun 2026 [S01] (S13's conflicting "UAE AI Act 2026" claim: see Excluded [S13]); the Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data was approved on 14 Jun 2026 [S16][S10].
- Why it matters to us: Public-sector deployment, not a rule binding our staff-only assistant (P1) or agent pilots (P3) (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: Medium — two Low-grade outlets without named authors agree [S06][S07] and match the official plan for a September 2026 start [S15].
