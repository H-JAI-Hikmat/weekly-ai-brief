## What changed this week

### 1. EU Scientific Panel meets on frontier AI loss-of-control risks — EU — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: The European Commission held a special meeting of its Scientific Panel on AI, attended by Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen [W01]. The panel has been investigating recent loss-of-control incidents, and the page reports no recommendations, decisions or non-compliance findings [W01]. Virkkunen said on 09 Oct 2026 that Europe is "well equipped" to deal with rogue AI agents [W02]. Background: the Commission surveyed more than 30 AI developers at the end of August and is analysing their responses [W02].
- Why it matters to us: Our agent pilots (P3) are the closest match to the topic. Direct EU obligations depend on whether we place AI on the EU market (Assumption P4). Nothing reported creates a new duty (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: High on the meeting (two sources agree: W01 High, W02 Medium). Outcomes are unknown.

### 2. ICO opens agentic AI call for evidence — UK — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: The Information Commissioner's Office (UK data protection regulator) opened a call for evidence on 08 Oct 2026, closing 20 Nov 2026 [W06]. It asks how data protection law applies to agentic AI's capabilities and risks, before final guidance is published [W06]. The survey covers data security, transparency, accountability, automated decision-making, fairness, purpose limitation and lawful processing [W06].
- Why it matters to us: Our pilot agents can call internal tools (P3), so the topics overlap with our work (Inference). Whether UK law applies to us is unconfirmed (see "For human decision").
- Action: Head of IT Risk · decide whether to submit a response, once Legal answers the applicability question · by 06 Nov 2026.
- Confidence: High — primary regulator page.

### 3. UAE bodies expand government agentic AI programmes — UAE — Relevance: Low
- What happened: Dubai's Agentic AI for Government Services Accelerator started its first phase with 33 Dubai Government entities assessing more than 300 public-facing use cases [W08]. The Dubai Electronic Security Centre CEO cited the Information Security Regulation in Dubai and the AI Security Policy as frameworks for adopting agentic AI [W08]. Background: a WAM report published 11 Oct 2026 says the UAE announced a Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data, but it states no powers [W10].
- Why it matters to us: The programmes cover government entities, not private organisations (Inference). The Federal Authority's powers remain unsourced, so any future obligations are unknown.
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: Medium — W08 is Medium grade, and W10 (High) gives no announcement date or powers.
