## What changed this week

### 1. EU scientific panel meets on frontier AI loss-of-control incidents — EU — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: The Commission reported on 09 Oct 2026 a special meeting of its Scientific Panel on AI, which has been "investigating recent loss-of-control incidents"; no recommendations or findings were reported [W01]. A Medium-grade report says Commission Executive Vice-President Henna Virkkunen [W01] called Europe "well equipped" to deal with what the outlet calls rogue AI agents [W02].
- Why it matters to us: Our agent pilots (P3) make such incidents relevant (Inference). Whether the AI Act reaches us depends on EU market presence (Assumption P4) (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: Medium — meeting confirmed by a High-grade source; no outcomes reported [W01].

### 2. ICO opens call for evidence on agentic AI and data protection — UK — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: The UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) opened a call for evidence (a public request for views) on 08 Oct 2026, closing 20 Nov 2026 [W06]. It asks how data protection law applies to agentic AI, before final guidance is published [W06].
- Why it matters to us: Our agent pilots (P3) are the kind of system in scope, but whether UK law applies to us is unconfirmed (Inference).
- Action: Head of Data & Analytics · decide whether to respond, once Legal confirms applicability · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: High — regulator's own page; applicability to us unknown [W06].

### 3. Dubai launches agentic AI accelerator for 33 government entities — UAE (Dubai) — Relevance: Low
- What happened: Gulf News reported on 05 Oct 2026 that 33 Dubai Government entities will assess more than 300 public-facing use cases in the Agentic AI for Government Services Accelerator [W08]. The Dubai Electronic Security Centre CEO cited two frameworks for adopting agentic AI [W08].
- Why it matters to us: It targets Dubai Government entities, so it likely does not bind us unless we supply them (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: Medium — single Medium-grade source; programme requirements not stated [W08].
