### 3. EU expert panel meets on AI loss-of-control incidents — EU — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: The Commission held a special meeting of its 60-expert Scientific Panel, which has been investigating recent loss-of-control incidents. No recommendations, decisions or findings are reported [W01]. Henna Virkkunen said on 09 Oct 2026 that Europe is "well equipped" for rogue AI agents [W02].
- Why it matters to us: Our agent pilots resemble the systems discussed (Inference). EU duties reach us only if we place AI on the EU market (Assumption P4).
- Action: Head of Information Security · complete the sandbox and logging check on agent pilots · by 19 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — no outcomes reported; incident details thin.

### 4. Dubai launches agentic AI accelerator for government — UAE — Relevance: Low
- What happened: Gulf News reported on 05 Oct 2026 that 33 Dubai Government entities will assess more than 300 public-facing use cases in the first phase of an agentic AI accelerator [W08]. The Dubai Electronic Security Centre CEO cited the Information Security Regulation in Dubai and the AI Security Policy as frameworks for adopting agentic AI [W08].
- Why it matters to us: This is a programme, not a new rule (Inference). The cited frameworks matter if we work with Dubai Government entities (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: Medium — one press source; policy texts not in pack.
