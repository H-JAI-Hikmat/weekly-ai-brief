## What changed this week

### 1. EU scientific panel meets on frontier AI loss-of-control incidents — EU — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: On 09 Oct 2026 the Commission reported a special meeting of its Scientific Panel on AI, which has been investigating recent loss-of-control incidents. No recommendations, decisions or findings were reported [W01]. Henna Virkkunen said Europe is "well equipped" for rogue AI agents [W02].
- Why it matters to us: Our agent pilots (P3) match the topic, but no EU requirement on us was reported (Inference). EU rules reach us only if we place AI on the EU market (Assumption P4).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: Medium — the official page reports no outcomes.

### 2. ICO opens call for evidence on agentic AI — UK — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: The ICO opened a call for evidence on 08 Oct 2026, closing 20 Nov 2026, on how data protection law applies to agentic AI [W06].
- Why it matters to us: Our agent pilots (P3) fall within the topic. It matters only if we process UK personal data (Inference); check with Legal.
- Action: Head of IT Risk · ask Legal whether to respond · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: High — primary ICO source; UK relevance to us is unconfirmed.

### 3. UAE report cites federal agentic AI programme and new AI authority — UAE — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: A WAM report published 11 Oct 2026 says the UAE announced the Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data, to integrate national data, AI and digital government systems [W10]. The federal Agentic AI Project aims to convert 50% of federal government sectors, services and operations to "autonomous, AI-driven models within two years" [W10].
- Why it matters to us: Federal direction may shape expectations of government suppliers (Inference).
- Action: Head of IT Risk · ask Legal to obtain primary texts · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — the report gives no powers for the authority.
