### 3. UAE report cites federal agentic AI programme and new AI authority — UAE — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: A WAM report published 11 Oct 2026 says the UAE announced the Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data, to integrate national data, AI and digital government systems [W10]. It adds that the federal Agentic AI Project aims to convert 50% of federal government sectors, services and operations to "autonomous, AI-driven models within two years" [W10]. The report gives no powers for the authority [W10].
- Why it matters to us: Federal direction on agentic AI may shape expectations of government suppliers (Inference). No obligation on us is shown.
- Action: Head of IT Risk · ask Legal to obtain primary texts for the authority and agentic framework · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — a High-grade source now supports the announcement; its date and the authority's powers are missing.
