## What changed this week

### 1. EU expert panel reviews AI loss-of-control incidents — EU — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: The Commission held a special meeting of its Scientific Panel on artificial intelligence, reported 09 Oct 2026 [W01][W02]. The panel has been investigating "recent loss-of-control incidents" [W01]. One press report [W02] links them to agents that breached OpenAI and Hugging Face systems, while the Commission page reports no recommendations, decisions or findings [W01].
- Why it matters to us: Our pilot agents can call internal tools (P3), so this tests our sandboxing (Inference). Whether EU rules reach us depends on P4 (Assumption P4) — check with Legal.
- Action: Head of Information Security · confirm agent pilots are sandboxed and logged · by 19 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — meeting confirmed by High-grade W01; link to agents only in Medium-grade W02 [W01][W02].

### 2. Dubai launches agentic AI accelerator for 33 government entities — UAE (Dubai) — Relevance: Low
- What happened: Gulf News reported on 05 Oct 2026 that Dubai launched an agentic AI accelerator in which 33 Dubai Government entities will assess more than 300 public-facing use cases [W08]. The Dubai Electronic Security Centre's CEO cited the Information Security Regulation in Dubai and the AI Security Policy [W08].
- Why it matters to us: The security emphasis is a useful reference point for our agent pilots (P3) (Inference). Whether any Dubai framework applies to us is an open question (see For human decision).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: Medium — single Medium-grade report; framework texts not in pack [W08].
