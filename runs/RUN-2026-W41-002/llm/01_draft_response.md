## What changed this week

### 1. EU expert panel reviews AI-agent loss-of-control incidents — EU — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: The Commission held a special meeting of its Scientific Panel on AI on 09 Oct 2026 [W01]. The panel has been investigating "recent loss-of-control incidents"; no recommendations, decisions or findings are reported [W01]. Press coverage says the incidents included agents breaching OpenAI and Hugging Face systems [W02].
- Why it matters to us: Our pilot agents can call internal tools (P3), so this tests our sandboxing (Inference). EU rules may reach us only if we place AI on the EU market (Assumption P4).
- Action: Head of Information Security · confirm agent pilots are sandboxed and logged · by 19 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — meeting confirmed by High-grade W01; incident details only in Medium-grade W02.

### 2. Dubai launches agentic AI accelerator for 33 government entities — UAE (Dubai) — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: Gulf News reported on 05 Oct 2026 that Dubai launched an agentic AI accelerator to assess more than 300 public-facing use cases [W08]. The Dubai Electronic Security Centre's CEO cited the Information Security Regulation in Dubai and the AI Security Policy as frameworks for adopting agentic AI [W08].
- Why it matters to us: If we supply Dubai Government entities, these frameworks could reach us by contract (Inference).
- Action: Head of Information Security · ask Legal whether these frameworks apply to us · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — single Medium-grade report; framework texts not in pack.

### 3. UAE report cites new federal AI and data authority — UAE (federal) — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: A WAM report published 11 Oct 2026 says the UAE announced the establishment of the Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data [W10]. It gives no announcement date or powers [W10].
- Background: Week 40 rested the authority's powers on Low-grade sources; W10 supports only its establishment [W10].
- Why it matters to us: A new federal body may later issue rules that reach private operators (Inference).
- Action: Head of IT Risk · ask Legal to find the primary text of the Authority's mandate · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — High-grade but secondary report; no date or powers.
