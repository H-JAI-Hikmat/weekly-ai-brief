## What changed this week

### 1. EU scientific panel meets on frontier AI loss-of-control risks — EU — Relevance: Medium
- What happened: A Commission press release dated 09 Oct 2026 reports a special meeting of its Scientific Panel on AI [W01]. The panel has been investigating recent loss-of-control incidents and was to present recommendations; none are reported [W01]. Henna Virkkunen, the Commission's Executive Vice-President for Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy [W01], said Europe is "well equipped" to deal with what Cryptopolitan calls "rogue AI agents" [W02].
- Why it matters to us: Later EU action on agent safety could reach our US model providers (Inference) (Assumption P2). Direct application to us may depend on our EU market role (Inference) (Assumption P4); check with Legal.
- Action: Head of IT Risk · confirm with Legal that we have no EU market exposure · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: High for the press release (primary source); no outcome is known.

### 2. ICO opens call for evidence on agentic AI — UK — Relevance: Low
- What happened: The UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) opened a call for evidence on 08 Oct 2026, closing 20 Nov 2026 [W06]. It asks how data protection law applies to agentic AI, to decide where to give more clarity before final guidance [W06].
- Why it matters to us: It could inform how our agent pilots (Assumption P3) are assessed for data protection, but only if UK law applies to us (Inference).
- Action: No action — monitor.
- Confidence: High — primary source.

### 3. Dubai launches agentic AI accelerator for 33 government entities — UAE (Dubai) — Relevance: Low
- What happened: The first phase brings together 33 Dubai Government entities to assess more than 300 public-facing use cases [W08]. The Dubai Electronic Security Centre CEO, Yousuf Hamad Al Shaibani, cited the Information Security Regulation in Dubai and the AI Security Policy as frameworks for adopting agentic AI [W08].
- Why it matters to us: It may signal local security expectations for agentic AI; whether they apply to us is unconfirmed (Inference).
- Action: Head of Information Security · ask Legal whether the AI Security Policy applies to us · by 30 Oct 2026.
- Confidence: Medium — single Medium-grade source.
